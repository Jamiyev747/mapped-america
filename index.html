Mapped America

America, explained with data.

Mapped America is a YouTube channel that turns official public data (U.S. Census Bureau, Bureau of Labor Statistics, Bureau of Economic Analysis, FHFA and others) into maps and charts about every U.S. state.

Watch the channel: youtube.com/@MappedAmericaData

About the "Mapped America Uploader" app

The uploader is a private tool used only by the channel owner. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload and schedule videos to the Mapped America channel. It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account.
