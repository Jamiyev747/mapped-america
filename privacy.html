Privacy Policy

Mapped America Uploader · Last updated: October 26, 2026

Who uses the app

The Mapped America Uploader is used only by the owner of the Mapped America YouTube channel to manage that channel. It is not available to the public.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publish times, and read basic information about the channel's own videos.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or process personal data from viewers or from any other Google account.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising.

Storage and security

The OAuth access token is stored only on the channel owner's private computer and server, with restricted file permissions, and is used only to manage the owner's own channel. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
